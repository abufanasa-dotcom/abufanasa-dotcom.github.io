Acoustics · Data quality / INDEPENDENT PROJECT

Interpreting noise data in context.

Prepare historical workplace measurements and compare dose definitions without generalizing investigation findings.

Code & documentation ↗
Original figure in repository ↗

01 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Implementation

  • Preserve data and quality flags; correct documented formula discrepancies.
  • Compare paired NIOSH and OSHA doses and analyze compatible subsets separately.

02 / METHODOLOGY

Data and approach

  1. Archived NIOSH HHE investigations, mainly 1996–2007, with selected cases through 2013.
  2. 807 personal and 582 area measurements; separate tables and flags for missing or problematic values.
  3. 758 valid NIOSH dose values; 680 valid paired NIOSH/OSHA measurements. These counts describe measurements, not necessarily distinct individuals.
  4. Only 33 records with suitable Leq and duration data support the separate German-context analysis. NIOSH dose values are not interchangeable with the daily noise exposure level LEX,8h.

03 / RESULT

Results in context

477 of 758 measurements meet or exceed the 100% NIOSH dose criterion.

Metrics from the documented analysis
NIOSH dose ≥ 100%477 / 758 (62.93 %)
NIOSH > OSHA in paired measurements672 / 680 (98.82 %)

In 672 of 680 paired measurements (98.82%), NIOSH dose exceeds OSHA PEL dose. Different definitions must be considered when interpreting the comparison.

SCOPE

Scope and limitations.

Investigation-based samples are not representative of all workplaces. The analysis is not a medical evaluation or certified assessment of German exposure limits.
