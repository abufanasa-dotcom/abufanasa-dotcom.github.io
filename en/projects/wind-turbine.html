Wind energy · Measurement data / INDEPENDENT PROJECT

Making power deviations visible.

Analyze historical wind-turbine SCADA data and identify sustained deviations for engineering review.

Code & documentation ↗Open dashboard ↗
Original figure in repository ↗

01 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Implementation

  • Screen operational events and data quality; compare a wind-speed reference with regression models.
  • Flag sustained deviations and inspect saved results through a Streamlit dashboard.

02 / METHODOLOGY

Data and approach

  1. 52,560 ten-minute timestamps from 2022, Kelmarsh turbine 1. Source: Kelmarsh wind farm data, Zenodo v4 (CC BY 4.0).
  2. Training January–August; model selection September–October; additional evaluation November–December. Whole-year exploratory analysis limits independence of the final period.
  3. Reference: median power in 0.5 m/s wind-speed bins. The ML model uses wind speed and direction to estimate contemporaneous power, not future production.
  4. Development-residual threshold: approximately 277.47 kW, with at least three consecutive ten-minute values.

03 / RESULT

Results in context

5 candidate events for engineering review.

Metrics from the documented analysis
Source timestamps52,560
Candidate events5

In the largest event, the mean model-relative deficit and integrated energy gap are approximately 350.88 kW and 350.88 kWh. Timestamps 02:10–03:00 UTC span 50 minutes; six ten-minute intervals represent 60 minutes. Exact interval boundaries remain unresolved.

SCOPE

Scope and limitations.

Deviations are not confirmed defects or proven recoverable energy losses. Individual context review of the other four events and external-year validation are outstanding in the project documentation.
